FRIENDS WITH STYLE — MOBILE RESPONSIVE UPDATE

What changed
- Phone product cards are now large and easy to view.
- Mobile product grid uses one card per row on normal phones.
- Header, menu, cart and buttons have larger touch targets.
- Hero image and text no longer look squeezed or tiny.
- Product popup, shopping bag and checkout now use the full phone screen.
- Colour, size and quantity controls are easier to tap.
- Maps, services, policy, contact and footer sections stack cleanly.
- Safe-area spacing added for iPhones with a notch/home indicator.
- Desktop and laptop styling remains unchanged.

Deployment
1. Back up the current hosted website folder.
2. Replace the existing website files with this full pack.
3. Upload every file and the complete assets folder.
4. Clear the browser cache or open the site in a private tab.

Mobile test checklist
- Open on iPhone and Android.
- Confirm there is no sideways scrolling.
- Open a product and select a colour and size.
- Add two different products to the bag.
- Open checkout and test Collection, Delivery and PAXI.
- Confirm the map and WhatsApp order buttons work.
