ORDER POLICY

Before placing an order.

Please read and accept the policy during checkout before your complete order is sent to WhatsApp.

01Deliveries are only done on Wednesdays and Saturdays.

02A 50% upfront payment is required when placing an order. The remaining balance is paid on delivery or collection.

03No refunds. Exchange only, subject to confirmation by the Friends With Style team.

04Nationwide delivery is available. Direct-delivery fees are charged according to distance.

05Orders remain open. Stock, production time and payment details are confirmed on WhatsApp.
